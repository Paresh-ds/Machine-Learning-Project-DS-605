1. PROJECT PURPOSE
======================================================================
Dataset: UCI Appliances Energy Prediction, recorded in a house in
Stambruges, Belgium (January–May 2016).

Our prediction target is:
    Total_Energy = Appliances + lights

For hourly data, Total_Energy is the SUM of available 10-minute Wh
measurements in each hour, NOT their average.

Data Mining Goal 1:
    Investigate unusually high energy consumption and distinguish
    plausible genuine events from possible erroneous measurements.

IMPORTANT: Statistical anomalies do not automatically indicate sensor
errors. Environmental associations or warming after energy use do not
prove the accuracy of individual energy readings.

Cold condition: T_out <= 10 deg C

Cold: 2,370 observations, 199 outliers, 8.3966% outlier rate

One-sided two-proportion Z-test, alternative: cold rate > warm rate
    Z = -3.2426
    p = 0.999408
    Decision: Fail to reject H0 at alpha = 0.05.

Observed rate was HIGHER in warm weather, contrary to the hypothesis.

Created cold-weather candidate flag (current preferred name):
    H1_Likely_Genuine_Candidate
199 observations met the cold-weather plus high-energy conditions.
This is only an environmental candidate flag, not a validated label.

7. HYPOTHESIS 3 — LARGE INDOOR/OUTDOOR TEMPERATURE DIFFERENCE
======================================================================
Large difference defined as greater than each floor's Q3.

Floor 1:
    Q3 = 16.79 deg C
    High difference: 822 observations, 57 outliers, 6.9343%
    Other difference: 2,467 observations, 253 outliers, 10.2554%
    Z = -2.8224; p = 0.997616
    Fail to reject H0 for the proposed positive association.

Floor 2:
    Q3 = 15.98 deg C
    High difference: 822 observations, 47 outliers, 5.7178%
    Other difference: 2,467 observations, 263 outliers, 10.6607%
    Z = -4.2007; p = 0.999987
    Fail to reject H0 for the proposed positive association.

Pearson correlations with Total_Energy:
    Floor_1_Temp_Diff: -0.1591
    Floor_2_Temp_Diff: -0.1750

A later clipped, nonnegative first-floor heating-gap proxy gave:
    Mean gap = 14.021 deg C
    Pearson r = -0.0970
    Spearman rho = -0.1591
    Mean energy (gap above median): 573.08 Wh
    Mean energy (gap at/below median): 644.66 Wh

These observations do not support the simple claim that larger
heating gap leads to higher recorded Appliances + lights energy.
Building heat loss can rise with temperature difference, but our
measured target does not necessarily include all heating-system energy.

By hour, the heating-gap Pearson correlations were positive at
18:00 (+0.2704), 19:00 (+0.3535), 20:00 (+0.3208), and
21:00 (+0.4536), as exploratory results.

11. MULTIVARIATE KNN DISTANCE ANALYSIS
======================================================================
Analyzed environmental unusualness using seven inputs:
    Floor_1_Temp
    Floor_2_Temp
    T_out
    Floor_1_Humidity
    Floor_2_Humidity
    RH_out
    Windspeed

Used StandardScaler fitted to normal-energy reference observations
(2,979 rows), then transformed the remaining high-energy cases.
Used KNN with five nearest normal-reference observations and mean
Euclidean distance in standardized feature space.

For the initial 107 cases, KNN distance statistics:
    Mean ~0.7283; median ~0.6812; max ~1.8034.

KNN identifies unusual environmental combinations; it is NOT a
validated classifier of true versus false energy readings.

A later run targeted 106 remaining Not_Reviewed high-energy outliers.
An illustrative export was proposed at:
    ../data/remaining_106_outliers.csv
with date, Total_Energy, T_out, KNN_Mean_Distance.
